What defines an authentic orthopedic mattress over conventional high-density foam? keyboard_arrow_down

Authentic orthopedic mattresses engineered by SleepExcellent feature targeted multi-zone resistance slabs. Conventional mattresses create uniform resistance, forcing the lumbar region into an arch while collapsing the hips. Our orthopedic cores utilize differential density layers to preserve neutral spinal alignment whether resting supine or lateral.

How does the 100-Night In-Home Trial Protocol function? keyboard_arrow_down

The physiological adaptation period for structural spinal realignment typically demands 21 to 30 nights. We insist you sleep on your selected slab for a minimum of 30 nights. If your orthopedic evaluation is unsatisfied within 100 calendar days of delivery, our logistics concierge collects the mattress free of charge with full reimbursement.

Can non-standard European, American, or vintage Indian bed dimensions be accommodated? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes. Every core is milled in our monolithic planar engineering plant. We cut to your exact inner-lip bed frame dimensions down to the millimeter without compromising edge encasement integrity or warranty coverage.
